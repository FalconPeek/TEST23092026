    classes) are now plain buttons: `flex min-h-11 items-center gap-1 rounded-full
    border border-primary bg-primary px-3 text-xs text-primary-foreground`. Removed the now
    -unused `Badge` import.

    lines inside a chip for a long attribute name, and `min-h` lets the chip grow instead of
    clipping.

    self-check, not explicitly named in T-021's or T-022's problem list.

    what actually matters since clicking anywhere in the label toggles a native `<input
    type="checkbox">`.
