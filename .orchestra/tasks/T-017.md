## Context
The tournament lifecycle: `draft` → `registration` → (generate) `in_progress` → `finished`. Actions (`lib/actions/tournaments.ts`):
- `setTournamentStatus({ tournamentId, groupId, status })`;
- `registerForTournament` / `unregisterFromTournament({ tournamentId, groupId })`: individual mode, members only (not spectators), status `registration`;
- `saveTournamentEntries({ tournamentId, groupId, entries: [{ name, seed?: number|null, playerIds }] })`: teams mode, admin, draft/registration;
- `generateTournamentBracket({ tournamentId, groupId })`: admin. It builds balanced teams first in individual mode, seeds by OVR unless every entry has a manual seed, then persists and flips the status to `in_progress`.

Team strength for the entries editor comes from `player_cards.ovr` (default 60).

   - `tabs.{overview,bracket,table,fixtures}` ("Resumen", "Llave", "Tabla", "Fechas").
2. `app/g/[groupId]/torneos/[tournamentId]/layout.tsx`:
   - Load the tournament (`notFound` if invisible). Render a header (name, format, status badge, entry mode, team size) and a tab bar of links.
   - Tabs: Resumen (always) · Llave (elimination formats + groups_ko) · Tabla (league, groups_ko, swiss) · Fechas (all, once in_progress).
3. `app/g/[groupId]/torneos/[tournamentId]/page.tsx` (Resumen):
   - **Admin controls by status:** draft → "Abrir inscripción"; draft/registration → "Generar fixture" (confirm dialog; disabled with `needTwoEntries` when <2 entries in teams mode, or <2 teams' worth of registrations in individual mode); in_progress → "Terminar torneo".
   - **Individual mode:** a list of registered players plus a register/unregister button for me (hidden for spectators).
   - **Teams mode:** `components/tournament/entries-editor.tsx` (`'use client'`, admin, draft/registration). It adds/renames/removes teams, assigns group players to teams (each player on ≤1 team), sets an optional manual seed, and shows the live avg OVR per team. "Guardar equipos" calls `saveTournamentEntries`. Non-admins see a read-only list of teams.
   - After generation: a short summary with a link to the Llave/Tabla tab.
4. Pure helper `lib/tournament/entries.ts` (assign/unassign/validate: unique players, non-empty names, unique seeds, avg OVR) with tests.
5. Tests: `lib/tournament/entries.test.ts` and `components/tournament/entries-editor.test.tsx`.
