## Context
After an admin runs `startReporting`, a match is in `reporting` with a `report_deadline` (48 h default) and a `rating_deadline` (72 h default).
- Team players report the score (`submitScoreReport`) and per-player stats (`submitStatReports`: goals, assists, own goals, saves for any team player, including themselves). Each reporter's newest report replaces their previous one (the RPC handles that).
- Team players, plus spectator participants when the group allows it (`spectators_can_rate`), rate every *other* team player 1–10 with up to 2 standout sub-attributes (`submitMatchRatings`).
- `get_match_report_summary(p_match_id)` → `[{ side, reporters, all_agree }]`: how many reporters per side and whether all score reports agree.
- Clients can only select their **own** reports/ratings (votes are anonymous); use those to prefill.

Reconciliation and finalization happen server-side later (FATHER's finalizer). This page is the per-match hub; T-015 adds the dispute/final-result sections to the same page.
